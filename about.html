About Mang Kulas

Our Story

Mang Kulas Water Refilling Station began with a simple idea: to provide clean, affordable, and accessible drinking water for our community.

As IT students, we imagined a business that combines our interest in technology with something people need every day. We chose a water refilling station because clean drinking water is important to every household.

Our goal is to provide quality water, friendly service, and convenient delivery for our customers. This project represents our idea of a business that puts customer needs first.
